What it does

A complete system, not a chat bubble.

Conversation
Natural-language AI

Understands what a visitor actually needs, in their own words, without a rigid menu of buttons.

Intent detection

Reads context and urgency to tell a browsing visitor apart from someone ready to buy.

Smart follow-up questions

Adapts its next question to the last answer instead of following a fixed script.

Lead scoring

Weighs intent, budget, timeline and engagement into a single 0–100 score.

Conversion
Contact capture

Collects name, phone and preferred contact method without feeling like a form.

Appointment booking

Offers open time slots directly in the conversation and confirms the booking.

CRM integration

Sends every qualified lead into the business's existing CRM automatically.

Automated follow-ups

Re-engages visitors who don't book right away with a timed follow-up sequence.

Insight
Conversation history

Every exchange is logged and searchable, so no context is lost between visits.

Lead dashboard

One view of every lead, its score, status and value across the business.

Priority notifications

Flags high-value leads the moment they're qualified, not at end of day.

Analytics

Tracks conversion, response time and lead source to show what's working.

How a visit becomes a booking

From anonymous visitor to appointment, automatically.

Each step happens inside a single conversation. Nothing is handed off to a form, a voicemail, or a "someone will call you back."

Visitor
Arrives on the website
AI conversation
Agent opens the dialogue and listens
Qualification
Service, location, timing and budget are established
Lead score
Conversation is scored 0–100 for intent and value
CRM
Qualified lead is pushed to the sales pipeline
Appointment
Available time slots are offered and booked
Follow-up
Automated sequence re-engages if no booking yet
