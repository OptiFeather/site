We treat land the way an architect treats a building — with structure, restraint, and an understanding that it has to work in twenty years, not just in photographs.

Every project begins the same way: on site, on foot, before a single line is drawn. Grade, drainage, light and existing growth decide the design — not the other way around.

01

Site first

Topography and soil dictate the plan, so the landscape sits naturally in its place rather than fighting it.

02

Built to age well

Stone, wood and concrete chosen for how they weather — landscapes should look better in ten years, not worse.

03

Quiet materials

A restrained palette lets the planting and the light do the work, instead of competing with it.

The Studio

A small studio of landscape architects, builders and gardeners.

Fieldwork was founded on a simple frustration: landscaping and landscape architecture had become two separate industries — one that draws, and one that builds — rarely speaking to each other.

We kept them under one roof. The people who design your landscape are the same people accountable for building it, and for the way it looks five years on.
