Since 2015

Started with one chair and a landlord who didn't believe in it.

Marcus opened The Steady Hand in the back third of a laundromat, one chair, no sign. Word got around the way it does in a neighborhood — not through ads, through Tuesday regulars telling Friday regulars. Nine years and five more chairs later, the standard hasn't moved: no phone at the chair, no rushing the line-up, no cut that walks out the door before it's right.

"A good barbershop isn't a service. It's a standing appointment with yourself." — Marcus, owner
